## Summary

210 MCQs, 42 short answers, 28 long answers and 21 paragraph builders were checked. Almost all match the book. **15 items** need a decision. None has a wrong answer key. The issues are:

- **Content not in the 15th edition (9 items).** Older Kotler editions taught the communication process, the six steps of effective communication, buyer-readiness stages, message appeals and patronage rewards. The 15th edition dropped them from Chapters 12 and 13. Keep these items if your lectures still teach these ideas. Otherwise hide them in the Question editor.
- **Wording that differs from the book (6 items).** These are small fixes.
